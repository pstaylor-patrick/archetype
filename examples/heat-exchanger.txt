Design a compact heat exchanger for cooling a 500W industrial laser module.

Requirements:
- Thermal load: 500W continuous, 750W peak (30 second bursts)
- Coolant: distilled water, flow rate 2-4 L/min available
- Inlet temperature: 20°C
- Maximum junction temperature: 65°C (laser diode limit)
- Envelope constraint: must fit within 150mm x 100mm x 50mm
- Pressure drop: less than 30 kPa at max flow
- Must interface with standard 1/4" push-to-connect fittings
- Material compatibility: no copper (coolant chemistry restriction)
- Target weight: under 1.2 kg
- Production volume: 2000 units/year
- Target cost: under $80 per unit at volume
- Must survive 10,000 thermal cycles without fatigue failure
