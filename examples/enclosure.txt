Design an IP67-rated enclosure for an outdoor IoT sensor node.

Requirements:
- Must house a PCB measuring 80mm x 60mm x 15mm
- Battery compartment for 2x 18650 lithium cells
- External antenna connector (SMA female bulkhead)
- Ambient temperature range: -20°C to +60°C
- Must survive a 1.5m drop onto concrete
- UV resistant (direct sunlight exposure, 10 year life)
- Must allow field battery replacement without tools (quarter-turn latch)
- IP67 ingress protection (temporary submersion to 1m)
- Target weight: under 250 grams (excluding battery and PCB)
- Production volume: 10,000 units/year
- Target cost: under $8 per unit at volume
- Must be mountable via DIN rail clip or zip-tie slots
- Color: neutral gray (RAL 7035 or similar)
