Design a mounting bracket for a satellite communication antenna.

Requirements:
- Must support a 15 kg antenna in a 3G vibration environment
- Operating temperature range: -40°C to +85°C
- Maximum allowable deflection under load: 0.5 mm
- Must be compatible with M8 bolt mounting pattern on a flat bulkhead
- Target weight: under 800 grams
- Production volume: 500 units/year
- Budget: under $45 per unit at volume
- Material must be non-magnetic (near sensitive RF equipment)
- Corrosion resistance required (outdoor marine environment)
- Design life: 15 years minimum
